# MSAI-631 Course Syllabus (Summary)

Course: MSAI-631-B02, Artificial Intelligence for Human-Computer Interaction.
University of the Cumberlands, 2026 Summer, Second Bi-term.

This course explores how artificial intelligence has impacted human-computer
interaction (HCI), from early punch cards and switches through modern virtual
reality and conversational interfaces. Major topics include usability
principles, interaction design, evaluation methods, and conversational agents.

The group project is worth a significant portion of the grade and consists of
five deliverables: (1) a project proposal of one to two pages, (2) a design
document of three to five pages, (3) the source code submitted to an
instructor-accessible git repository and as a zip archive, (4) a results paper
of five to seven pages, and (5) a presentation of at most 20 slides.

Projects must use free resources only. OpenAI and other paid APIs are not
allowed. Acceptable platforms include Hugging Face Spaces and Google
Colaboratory, and the project should be able to run locally on a typical
laptop. Late work follows the university's standard late policy: contact the
instructor before the deadline if you anticipate a problem.

Our group's project is a course assistant chatbot using retrieval-augmented
generation. The models used are google/flan-t5-base for generation and
all-MiniLM-L6-v2 for embeddings, with FAISS for similarity search and Gradio
for the chat interface, hosted on a Hugging Face Space.
